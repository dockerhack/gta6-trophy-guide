An offline, single-page trophy guide and tracker for all 55 Grand Theft Auto VI PlayStation 5 trophies — the story spine, the 100% checklist grind, the missable windows, and every disagreement between the sources, shown both ways.

## What it is

- **Ledger** — a checklist of all 55 trophies with earned tracking, a detail panel for each trophy (official description, "in short", numbered steps, tips, sources), a sources panel, and a next-trophy strip.
- **Roadmap** — five stages from the story spine to the platinum, the four ground rules every source agrees on, and the missable watch list.
- **Missables** — the 10 flagged trophies (2 possibly missable, 8 at risk) and the 45 safe after the credits.
- **Source Matrix** — 55 trophies against the 10 most-cited source pages, with a flag for every dispute.
- **100% Ledger** — the 17 checklist counters (vehicles, animals, stunts, challenges, meditations, money and more), 15 save slots, the full disagreement ledger, and export/import.

## How it was built

65 source pages across 22 sites, read 20 Nov 2026. Every claim in the guide is cited: 490 claims, 466 backed by two or more sources, 55 of 55 guides citing three or more sources. Where the sources disagree, the guide shows both sides and states its call — 24 disputes in all. PSNProfiles and TrueAchievements refused full access and are not cited.

## Run it

Static files under `app/` — open `app/index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder with any static server. No build step, no network requests. Progress is saved in the browser's local storage and can be exported as JSON or shared as a progress link.
